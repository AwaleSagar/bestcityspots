## Design Context

### Users

Best City Spots serves deliberate travelers, city explorers, remote planners and curious readers who want to choose where to go with confidence. They use it while dreaming, comparing destinations, planning practical details, or checking live conditions on the way.

### Brand Personality

Calm, intelligent and trustworthy. The interface should feel like a well-edited city almanac: literate, data-rich but quiet, and honest about where every fact comes from.

### Aesthetic Direction — "Editorial almanac"

Warm paper and ink neutrals, one deep harbor-blue accent and a sparing marigold highlight. Newsreader (serif) for display type, Geist (sans) for the interface, tabular numerals for data. Hairline rules and generous spacing instead of glass, blur, gradients or drop shadows. Layouts lead with typography and data, because cities carry no photography. Light and dark themes are equal citizens. Tokens: `docs/design-tokens.md`; motion: `docs/motion-policy.md`.

### Design Principles

1. Design for traveler intent: discovery (home, guides), decision (city guide, compare) and planning (saved places, day plans) each have a clear home.
2. Make sources visible: every AI-written block is labelled and dated; every live number shows its provider and age.
3. Structure through typography and rules, not decoration: one section pattern, one card pattern, one list-row pattern, reused everywhere.
4. Motion only confirms change; nothing loops or reveals on scroll.
5. Inclusive by default: WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, keyboard-first interactions, 44px touch targets, device-local personal data, and fast, image-free first paint.
