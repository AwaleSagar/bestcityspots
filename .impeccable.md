## Design Context

### Users

Best City Spots serves deliberate travelers, city explorers, remote planners, and curious readers who want to choose where to go with confidence. They use the product while dreaming, comparing destinations, planning practical details, or orienting themselves around a city with live context, AI-assisted briefings, weather, metrics, and curated places.

### Brand Personality

Calm, intelligent, and hyperlocal. The interface should feel like a refined organic atlas: premium but accessible, data-rich but quiet, emotionally resonant without becoming cinematic hype.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use an editorial, biophilic travel intelligence language: warm tinted neutrals, literary display typography, fluid organic shapes, subtle terrain/leaf/water cues, transparent source labeling, and purposeful motion. Preserve the existing light-first liquid glass system, support dark mode, and avoid generic neon tech dashboards, overused glassmorphism, or rigid card grids.

### Design Principles

1. Design for traveler intent first: discovery, comparison, planning, and on-trip context should each have clear UI responses.
2. Make intelligence feel human: AI, live data, and sources should be visible, trustworthy, and written in plain language.
3. Let layouts breathe organically: use fluid type, intrinsic grids, varied spacing, asymmetry, and component-level responsiveness.
4. Add sensory polish with restraint: motion and visual effects should guide attention, respect reduced motion, and never overpower readability.
5. Keep the experience premium and inclusive: accessible contrast, keyboard/touch usability, privacy-conscious location behavior, and fast loading are part of the brand.
